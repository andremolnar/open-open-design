# Maintainer of this fork

[Andre Molnar (@andremolnar)](https://github.com/andremolnar) maintains this independent fork. Contributors and the original [OpenDesign authors](README.md#credit-to-the-original-authors) are credited for their work; they do not maintain this fork by virtue of those contributions.

There is no core team, external-maintainer program, paid Fellow program, or guaranteed review or release schedule for this repository. Issues and pull requests are welcome on [this fork](https://github.com/andremolnar/open-open-design/issues), but decisions about its direction and merges rest with me. The original project's governance and roadmap do not apply here; see the [README](README.md#direction-of-this-fork) for this fork's current direction.
